menu_bookAnswer from 2 help articles

Each charge on your invoice maps to a plan or add-on that was active during the billing period, so a second charge usually means a teammate added a seat or add-on partway through the month. Partial months are prorated automatically.1 Check the itemized breakdown on the invoice in the Billing tab to see what each charge covers.

If the charges still don't match what you signed up for, submit a request. Incorrect billing is handled with our fastest response time.2
